In this project, the artists jointly explore the cycles and mysteries of cosmic evolution, forging a connection between the infinite and the intimate. Through a collaborative dynamic, they create unique works reflecting their perspectives and the perfect symbiosis between them, resulting in a new and unique expression.

Through symbolic rings, they delineate different stages that capture special moments in the universe's life, ultimately the essence of our existence. Each ring opens a window to a unique moment in the universe's expansion. Beginning with the creation of matter, through understanding and conceptualizing how we got to where we are today in the evolutionary process, and then looking into the future of evolutionary consciousness.

The works continue to expand from the initial 297 x 420 mm (A3) format, then to 420 x 594 mm (A2), and finally to the third ring in an imposing 1500 x 900 mm format, reflecting in their growth the vast expansion of the universe they explore.

This is how we navigate the intricate path of our existence, understanding that each stage is reached through an interplay of countless factors. The Universe is a tapestry that folds the vast and immense into the minute and microscopic, where the infinite flows seamlessly between the expansive and the intimate. In this journey, "Sabiduría Universal" invites us to reflect on the interconnections of all things and the profound unity that underpins the cosmos.
