Mauricio Salatino, also known as Maryland Supreme (born in Argentina, an Argentinian/Italian/British citizen currently living in London, UK) spent most of his professional life as a software engineer working on building the cloud and open-source tools. His art exploration started early in high school when he chose an art-oriented track instead of science for some strange reason. This artistic exploration significantly hindered his math and scientific skills when he started his engineering career. Still, it was the beginning of a deeper exploration that never stopped.

Over the last 10+ years (2013 - 2025), Maryland Supreme has been creating and exploring the interconnections between the natural world and the unknown. This exploration into the visual realm connects familiar objects and entities and frames them with abstract concepts and contradicting messages to promote broader interpretations.
